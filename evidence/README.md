# Evidence

Education, employment, certifications, leadership/volunteering, portfolio, and
other supporting material are preserved under this evidence area. The visible
working categories are intentionally broad; historical nesting is retained only
where it protects provenance or avoids filename collisions.
Reusable resumes and cover letters live at the workspace root; historical
opportunity-specific submissions are retained under `../old/`.

Subfolders preserve historical versions and provenance. A location, filename,
or modification date does not make a document authoritative. See
[`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) before selecting, editing, renaming, or replacing a
career document.

The [resume area](../resumes/README.md) now has a deliberate exception to that
general rule: `../resumes/current/fp&a/docx/` and `../resumes/current/fp&a/pdf/`
contain Leo's current FP&A DOCX/PDF pair;
older versions remain under `../resumes/old/`. Leo completed his manual edits
on 2026-09-18; the revised wording and PDF layout were reviewed for John's email.
