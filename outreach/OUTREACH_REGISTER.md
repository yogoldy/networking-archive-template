## Maintenance rules

- Keep the register and each person's Snapshot aligned.
- Store evidenced contact routes in `Contact method`, using `Email:
  name@example.com`, `Phone: +1 ...`, or `Unknown`. When multiple addresses
  are evidenced, retain each with the most recently evidenced address clearly
  identified. Person notes link to this register rather than repeating mutable
  addresses or phone numbers.
- Use relationship paths in this form: `Mutual connection — [context]`,
  `Existing relationship — [context]`, `Alumni connection — [school/program]`,
  `LinkedIn — cold`, `Email — cold`, `Other — [brief]`, or `Unknown`.
- Include the relevant date in a current status after contact begins. Sent,
  received, replied, and meeting-scheduled status requires Leo's confirmation
  or actual correspondence evidence.
- Store actual messages and conversations in the person note. Store explicit
  actions once in [Leo To-Do](../docs/LEO_TODO.md#Outreach), linked both ways.
  Passive waiting is status context, not a task.
- Never send a message, schedule a meeting, or alter an external record merely
  because a person appears here.
