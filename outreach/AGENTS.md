# Outreach instructions

These rules supplement the root `AGENTS.md`.

- Never send a message, contact a person, schedule a meeting, or update an
  external contact record without Leo's explicit instruction.
- A draft is not evidence of sending. Record `Sent` only from sent-message
  evidence or Leo's direct confirmation.
- Reverify time-sensitive roles and employers before proposed outreach.
- Read the register and applicable person note, then relevant career evidence
  linked from the Leo profile at `docs/PROJECT_MEMORY.md`. Choose accomplishments for Leo's actual
  request; no required narrative formula. Keep register/person Snapshot fields
  aligned.
- Record only actual correspondence and conversations under dated headings in
  the person file. Put useful internal context under Preparation context.
  Preserve sent wording; unsent drafts may remain in chat and do not establish
  contact.
- Always consider whether correspondence requires action from Leo. Automatically
  capture warranted actions once in [Leo To-Do](../docs/LEO_TODO.md#Outreach),
  linked to supporting person-note evidence, with a Next steps backlink.
  If no action remains, Next steps is `None.`; preserve unaffected outstanding
  actions. Follow the [action review](../docs/OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md#Correspondence-action-review).
- Protect private contact information and expose only what the task requires.
- Preserve correspondence and introduction provenance; do not overwrite,
  rename, merge, or delete source material without specific authorization.
- After every successfully completed Mail reconciliation, replace
  `docs/OUTREACH_SCAN_UPDATE.md` with the latest scan date, accounts, method,
  external-action boundary, and result. If nothing new matched, remove the
  prior correspondence delta and state that no new correspondence was found.
  If messages matched, use person-by-person `Evidence`, `Record changes`, and
  `Deliberate no-change` sections. Report every automatic task and supporting
  evidence/question-link change as applied; distinguish proposed changes.
  Other canonical reconciliation still requires approval. Before finishing,
  check affected questions, tasks, reciprocal links, and scan reporting.
