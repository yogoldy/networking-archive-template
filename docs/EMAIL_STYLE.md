# Email style

These rules are confirmed by the 2026-09-16 legacy ChatGPT handoff unless
marked otherwise. Apply them as defaults, then adapt to the actual recipient,
relationship, and objective.

**Confirmed — Leo, 2026-09-18:** Use accomplishments flexibly for the actual
request. The defaults below are writing guidance, not a predefined story
formula. Preserve established ownership (including model creation), while
keeping claims within their evidence boundaries. Do not substitute generic
AI/governance language for the financial accomplishment being discussed.

## Voice and structure

- Default to concise, direct, polished, warm, conservative, and professional.
- Preserve Leo's purposeful wording and framing instead of mechanically
  replacing it.
- “I hope this message finds you well” is a historically confirmed default
  opening, not a mandatory phrase when context calls for something more direct.
- For a warm introduction, name the connector immediately and acknowledge what
  the connector already shared.
- Include only enough background to establish relevance—normally school/major
  plus one or two strong experiences. Do not restate the resume.
- Make a modest, low-friction ask and show scheduling flexibility.
- Close with “Best,” and “Leo” unless the situation or an approved signature
  block calls for something else.
- Use conservative formatting and avoid unnecessary bolding.

## Syllogistic compression

**Confirmed by Leo, 2026-09-19:** Prefer writing that lets the reader infer the
point from a compact sequence rather than spelling out every connection. A
personal fact should establish relevance, and the relevance should lead
naturally to the ask. Each sentence should do one useful job; do not stack
multiple generic claims merely to sound thorough.

The tone can become warmer while getting shorter when the logic is specific:

**Earlier, more generic version**

> I’d be happy to meet and tell you more about my background and the kinds of
> roles I’m exploring. I’m happy to work around your schedule.

**Leo’s preferred revision**

> I’d be happy to meet and tell you more about my background and the kinds of
> roles I’m exploring. I appreciate your willingness to connect and am glad to
> work around your schedule, so feel free to suggest some times that work best
> for you.

The second sentence combines appreciation, flexibility, and a natural next step
without turning them into separate instructions.

The same principle applies to relevance:

**Weaker connection**

> I’m currently based between Clearwater and Boynton Beach, and I’m open to
> opportunities in the Tampa Bay and Miami areas as well as remote roles.

**Stronger connection**

> I’m currently based between Clearwater and Boynton Beach (which is about an
> hour north of Miami), so I’m open to opportunities in the Tampa Bay and Miami
> areas as well as remote roles.

The location explains the geographic interest; the reader does not need a
separate sentence explaining why the locations matter.

## Editing checks

- Remove adjacent repetition.
- Do not over-defend a finance-to-consulting transition or introduce a mismatch
  objection the recipient did not raise.
- Preserve concrete feedback exactly when it matters; avoid euphemistic spin.
- Make personalization factual: a referral, role, desk, school tie, or specific
  point actually discussed.
- Make helping easy for senior contacts: provide forwardable material, attach
  what was requested, and make a call optional when appropriate.
- Prefer one polished recommendation. Offer variants only when a real strategic
  choice exists. This is inferred from prior workflow, not a universal rule.

## Subject lines

- Identify Leo and the purpose plainly.
- A historically approved form was: “Leo Goldberg – Background & Quick
  Overview of Roles I’m Exploring.”
- Role- or desk-specific subject lines may name relevant experience when that
  improves routing.
- Avoid clever, vague, or sales-oriented subject lines.
