## Source authority

Approved on 2026-09-16:

1. Leo's latest dated direct statement controls preferences, intent, and
   present-status corrections.
2. A current, role-appropriate primary document or tracker controls its own
   facts and workflow.
3. Sent-message, application, or screenshot evidence controls whether an
   external action happened.
4. The reviewed legacy ChatGPT handoff supports prior context and historical
   decisions but does not prove current status.
5. Agent inference is last and must be labeled.

Preserve source conflicts and stale historical statements. Do not convert
absence of evidence into a negative fact.
